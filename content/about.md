<p class="about-lede site-reveal">I’m a systems-minded builder who likes getting close enough to a problem to see how the pieces fit. I move comfortably between requirements, code, documentation, infrastructure, and the people who have to use the result.</p>

<p class="about-lede site-reveal site-reveal-delay-1">My work spans software, infrastructure, security, and operations. I enjoy following a request all the way through the system—from the first question to the practical outcome.</p>

<p class="about-lede site-reveal site-reveal-delay-2">My MBA helps me connect technical decisions to cost, controls, customer outcomes, and adoption. It gives me a useful business vocabulary without losing sight of the work itself.</p>

<section class="about-mission-card site-reveal" aria-labelledby="mission-heading">
